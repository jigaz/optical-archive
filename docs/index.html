How to burn a disc, kept for whoever needs it next.

Standards, drive command sets, file-system specifications and open-source burning software for CD, DVD and Blu-ray. When a document’s license lets us, we keep a verbatim copy with its checksum. When it doesn’t, we link to the original and ask the Internet Archive to snapshot it.

This archive tracks 82 items, 41 of which may be copied. A monthly job re-checks every link, downloads fresh copies, records any that changed, and publishes them as a release.

Talking to drives

The command sets software sends to CD, DVD and Blu-ray drives over SCSI, ATAPI, SATA and USB. MMC is the standard; the Mt. Fuji specification is the industry document it grew out of.

ItemOriginal and licenseHere
SNIA INF-TA-1010: Mt. Fuji Commands for Multimedia Devices (successor of SFF INF-8090, the Mt. Fuji specification)
The drive-maker industry document that MMC was built from. It covers BD, DVD and CD recording commands and is the freely available alternative to buying MMC.		SNIA SFF TA TWG
